## 4. Known items, not fixed (by design or out of scope)

- **LCP 3.5 s on throttled mobile.** Still a client-rendered SPA: ~0.9 s of script evaluation under the 4× CPU slowdown before anything can paint, plus the hero's entrance animation. The real fix remains build-time prerendering, which needs `localStorage`/`navigator` guards in the providers and `hydrateRoot`. Desktop LCP is well under 2.5 s.
- **react-pdf 4.5**: a `render` callback inside an absolutely positioned, `fixed` footer is dropped, so the ATS footer prints the update date without page numbers (the designed CV keeps them in its in-flow header).
- Virtrify bullets describe the agreed scope, not results: update them once the first workflows are live.
